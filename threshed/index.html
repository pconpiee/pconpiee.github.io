Why it's called Threshed

Separate the seed
from the chaff.

Threshing is the old work of the harvest: you beat the cut stalk until the true seed falls free of the chaff — the husk, the dust, everything that isn't the grain.

Most people at a turning point are carrying both at once: a real gift tangled up with fear, other people's expectations, and a dozen wrong directions. It's hard to tell the seed from the husk when it's your own.

The work is simple to name and hard to do alone: separate the seed from the chaff — then plant it where it can grow.
