The Thread

I grew up far from home — a German school, club soccer in Italy, a high-school diploma in Alaska. I’ve been the foreigner in the room most of my life.

So I feel most at home in international space — among people who are also far from home.

I keep trying to go into full-time ministry. God keeps sending me back into the work — the Air Force, a war-zone hospital in Ethiopia, a startup in Houston, satellite software, the International Space University, a deep-tech MBA, and now venture and mentoring founders. I stopped calling it a detour. The people I work with are the people I’m sent to.

I’d rather you see what I believe than hear me argue it. The teacher I follow was equal to God and still sat with Romans and fishermen, scholars and the overlooked — each in their own language. Be present, tell the truth, let love carry the rest.

Addis Ababa Strasbourg The Air Force Leanspace ISU Tel Aviv Friday nights · 10–20 nations

So these tools aren’t the ministry — they’re the excuse for presence. Someone comes for a CV or a fundraising plan and leaves having been seen. I build them for free, for the person who’s the most capable in the room and the least convinced they belong there.

The Road, So Far

Where the work has taken me

2012 – 2016
US Air Force Academy
Commissioned an officer; B.Sc. in Geospatial Science; competed and instructed on Wings of Blue, the US Air Force Parachute Team. Where the discipline was built and the calling got loud.
2016 – 2021
Space Operations
Five years a Space Operations Officer. Helped run the $17.5B US Space Surveillance Network and trained 240+ operators through the Space Force stand-up.
2021
Ethiopia
A teaching hospital in the Tigray war. I went to be a missionary; I came home knowing the field was wider than I’d thought.
2022
Houston
Co-founded a consulting startup. It failed — and taught me the difference between a good idea and a viable business better than any win could.
2023 – 2025
Leanspace
Built a French satellite-software startup’s US defense business from zero — launched two product lines, co-authored ~€14.5M in grants, and built the partnerships that became its Series A.
2025 – present
International Space University
Run the commercial side — sovereign partnerships, executive programs, and new ventures — for the world’s oldest space-education institution.
2026 – 2027
Coller Deep-Tech MBA — Tel Aviv
Inaugural cohort. Moving toward venture, M&A, and mentoring deep-tech founders — building places great founders come from.
Always
The local church
Elder at a local church; small-group host. Friday nights: 10–20 people from 6–13 nations around one table. The thing the résumé never quite captures.
